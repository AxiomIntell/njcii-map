About NJ-CII

New Jersey Critical Industries Initiative

The New Jersey Critical Industries Initiative (NJ-CII) is a nonprofit effort to map, analyze, and strengthen New Jersey's critical industrial base. Focused on four key sectors — Defense & Aerospace, Life Sciences & Pharmaceutical Manufacturing, Energy & Critical Infrastructure, and Advanced Manufacturing & Logistics — NJ-CII provides the data infrastructure that economic development, workforce, and national security stakeholders need to make informed decisions.

The Industrial Base Map is NJ-CII's core analytical tool. It provides an interactive, searchable, filterable view of New Jersey's critical industries ecosystem — enabling stakeholders to identify firms by sector, geography, contract value, certifications, and NAICS code. This is not a static directory; it is a living intelligence platform designed to support economic development strategy, supply chain analysis, and workforce planning.

This Tier 2 release adds readiness scoring, trend analysis, cross-agency intelligence, gap analysis, SBIR/STTR award tracking, and enhanced firm profiles with verification status. The dataset now includes 351 firms drawn from multiple federal data sources.

Small Business Innovation Research & STTR award data
Manual Research
Major prime contractor facilities and installations

Disclaimer

This is a prototype. Data is derived from publicly available federal sources and may not be comprehensive or fully current. Contract values represent obligations, not disbursements. Firms flagged as "Entity-Wide" have contract values that reflect nationwide totals, not NJ-specific activity. Firm sector classifications are approximations based on primary NAICS codes and contract types. Readiness scores are algorithmic assessments, not official ratings.
